the grammar every surface obeys

Header block

  • [colour] [name / subline] [accent action] [⋯] on every surface. The accent slot is the surface's output: New round, Export, Take off, Save PDF.
  • Secondary and destructive commands live in ⋯ and nowhere else. No footer bars, no row-face buttons.

Numbers

  • Three fixed slots, Area · Perimeter · Count, on round and scope. Unlit = not produced.
  • Mono tabular figures everywhere; unit in 11px ink-3 after the value; a dash for none.

State

  • One InfoBar per surface, info / warning / success, at most one action.
  • Row state is an 11px word under the row's number, coloured only when it needs attention.

Rows and type

  • Navigable items are 44px two-line ListView rows with a chevron.
  • Data rows are 23–24px. Section heads are 28px Body Strong with the count in ink-3 and one command or aside on the right.
  • Type is 12px, 14px for the surface name, 17px for the three quantities. Nothing else.

Fields

  • WinUI compact TextBox and ComboBox, 24px, label left. Units ride inside the field.
  • Free text a person writes (cover, notes) is a filled multi-line box at the same rule.

Words

  • Sheet numbers, never filenames. Commit, never Lock. No word twice on one screen.
